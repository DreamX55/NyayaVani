# NyayaVani (न्यायवाणी) — Product Context

## Purpose
NyayaVani is a voice-first, vernacular sovereign legal co-pilot and incident intake suite built for India. It translates raw, colloquial, spoken citizen grievances (in Hindi, Marathi, English, and regional Indic languages) into immediate statutory literacy, actionable legal next steps, and formal legal representations/notices.

## Target Audience
- **Grassroots Indian Citizens**: Street vendors facing municipal eviction, daily wage laborers facing wage theft, gig workers, citizens in police custody or check-post harassment, victims of cyber loan app blackmail.
- **Hardware & Context Constraints**: Used primarily on budget Android smartphones ($100–$200 phones), frequently outdoors under bright sunlight, in noisy streets, or on shared family devices.
- **Literacy & Demographics**: Low formal or legal literacy; high comfort speaking in native mother tongue (Hindi, Marathi, Hinglish); discomfort navigating complex bureaucratic portals or reading English legal PDFs.

## Core Problem
When a street vendor's cart is seized or a worker's wages are withheld, they do not know:
1. Whether what happened was legal or illegal.
2. What specific section or protection (Dhaara) protects them (e.g., Street Vendors Act 2014, Section 3(3); BNS Section 303).
3. The exact immediate steps to take (filing a Zero FIR, notifying DLSA, calling 112/1930).
4. How to format a written formal notice or complaint.

## Core Jobs-to-be-Done
1. **Listen & Understand**: Allow the user to tap once and speak freely in their mother tongue without feeling intimidated.
2. **Context-Aware Dialogue**: Intelligently ask 1–2 relevant missing facts (Date, Location, Accused party, Notice served) instead of rigid questionnaires.
3. **The 1-Minute Verdict**: Plain-language voice explanation of why the action violated the law and which statutory Dhaara apply.
4. **Actionable Checklist**: Step-by-step chronological action items with emergency helplines.
5. **Instant Formal Draft**: Generate a structured, verifiable formal legal notice/complaint ready to copy, download, and review with an advocate.
