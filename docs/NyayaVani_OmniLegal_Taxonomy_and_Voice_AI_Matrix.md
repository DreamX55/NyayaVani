### High-Value Workflows & Commercial Architecture

1. **Autonomous Motor Accident & Tort Response:**
   - Hands-free voice intake post-collision.
   - Gathers GPS, license plates, dashcam footage, and party declarations.
   - Checks compliance with Section 134 Motor Vehicles Act, 2019.
   - Instantly compiles and transmits the First Accident Report (FAR) and General Insurance Council (GIC) claim intimation.
2. **Real Estate Title & RERA Diligence Co-Pilot:**
   - Ingests scanned or spoken property registry details.
   - Audits mother deeds, encumbrance certificates, occupancy certificates (OC), and building plan sanctions.
   - Identifies non-compliance with the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA).
   - Generates an actionable Title Search Report (TSR) flagging agricultural conversion issues and litigation caveats.
3. **Voice-Assisted Commercial Contract Redlining:**
   - Executive dictates business terms or objections verbally in natural speech.
   - System analyzes contract clauses (Indemnity, Limitation of Liability, Governing Law, Non-Compete) against Indian Contract Act, 1872.
   - Automatically outputs redlined docx drafts with risk score indicators.
4. **Estate Planning & Wealth Succession:**
   - Voice-guided interview deconstructing family tree, ancestral vs. self-acquired assets under Hindu Succession Act, 1956 / Indian Succession Act, 1925.
   - Generates legally unambiguous Wills, Codicils, and Family Settlement Deeds with biometric/voice execution instructions.
